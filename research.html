GIS (Raleigh City)

Overview

City-scale 3D building models of Raleigh, North Carolina, organized by Level of Detail (LoD):

  • LOD 1 – block models: each building footprint extruded to a single roof height.
  • LOD 2 – building models with roof shapes.
  • LOD 3 – detailed building models with facade elements such as windows.

GIS (Raleigh City)

LOD 1

Raleigh LoD1 3D Building Model

A Level-of-Detail 1 (LoD1) block model of every building within the City of Raleigh (about 156,000 buildings). Each building footprint is extruded to its roof height measured from the 2024 QL1 airborne LiDAR; where LiDAR heights were unavailable (about 11% of buildings), heights are estimated from floor counts. The model serves as the geometric base for city-scale building energy modeling.
